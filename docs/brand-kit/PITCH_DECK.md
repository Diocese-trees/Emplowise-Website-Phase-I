# Emplowise pitch deck, 2026

Eight slides, one question per slide, in the order a buyer or partner actually
asks them. The deck is written for Office: one headline of nine words or fewer,
three or four blocks whose answers stay under eighteen words so Arial or Calibri
cannot reflow them into a fifth line. Every unresolved number is in the speaker
notes, never on the slide.

**Grounds.** Navy `#081B3E`, white `#FFFFFF`, mist `#F0F9F9` or `#F7F7F7`, deep
teal `#0F766E`. Reverse lockup on navy and deep teal, primary lockup on white and
mist.

**Counter.** `01 / 08` through `08 / 08`, bottom right, small print.

---

## Slide 01: Product intro

**Slide Goal.** In one screen, say what Emplowise is, who it serves and why it
exists.

**Approved Ground and Layout.** Navy `#081B3E` full bleed, reverse lockup top
left, headline upper third, three WH blocks in a single row beneath, counter
bottom right. Nothing else on the slide.

**On-Slide WH Content.**

Headline: *Remote HR structure for growing Pakistani businesses*

- **What.** A remote HR team that installs and then runs payroll, performance and
  policy.
- **Who.** Startups and SMEs in Pakistan, five to one hundred employees, with no
  senior HR hire.
- **Why.** Published prices, written scope, one named team, and a free four-stage
  audit.

**Speaker Notes.**

- Emplowise is a service, not software. The buyer keeps their systems and gains a
  named HR owner.
- The category is "remote HR services in Pakistan"; the differentiator is that
  the price and the method are published.
- Two audiences: B2B buyers on retainers, and early career candidates on
  EmploStart for free.
- Open with the buyer's situation, not the company history. The first sentence
  should be about their payroll calendar.

**Evidence Status.** Category, delivery model and pricing are tier 5 confirmed
business facts in `config/emplowise.php`; no external statistic is used.

## Slide 02: Problem

**Slide Goal.** Make the cost of informal HR concrete before presenting a
service.

**Approved Ground and Layout.** White `#FFFFFF`, primary lockup top left, four
blocks in a two-by-two card grid, 1 px `#E4EAF1` borders, navy headline. No
imagery.

**On-Slide WH Content.**

Headline: *Informal HR breaks at thirty staff*

- **What fails.** Contracts, payroll inputs and leave live in memory or a
  spreadsheet.
- **Who feels it.** The founder becomes the HR helpdesk and the escalation
  point.
- **Where.** Pakistani startups and SMEs growing faster than their documentation.
- **Why alternatives fail.** Agencies deliver reports, senior hires cost a
  salary, and neither runs the process.

**Speaker Notes.**

- Ask the room which of the four failure modes they recognise. The answer tells
  you which tier to quote.
- A single payroll error or missed confirmation is usually the trigger event, not
  headcount alone.
- Avoid demographic framing. The buyer is defined by the situation, not by age or
  city tier.
- If asked for data: Emplowise has no published baseline yet, so cite the
  structural argument rather than an invented percentage.

**Evidence Status.** Tier 5 internal belief, consistent with the buyer situations
on the website. No market statistic is claimed.

## Slide 03: Solution

**Slide Goal.** Show that the answer is a repeatable method with written outputs,
not a personality.

**Approved Ground and Layout.** Mist `#F0F9F9`, four-step horizontal strip with
teal connectors, headline above, one-line output under each step, primary lockup
top left.

**On-Slide WH Content.**

Headline: *One method, four stages, written outputs*

- **What.** Assess, identify, improve and review the client's people operations.
- **How.** Audit the gap, name owners, install the process, then review it
  monthly.
- **Why it wins.** Every stage ends in a document the client keeps and can run.

**Speaker Notes.**

- Stage outputs: gap map, prioritised fix list, action plan with owners, running
  processes, quarterly review pack.
- The method is identical for a 12-person startup and an 80-person SME; only the
  depth changes.
- Say plainly what Emplowise does not do: no legal advice as a law firm, no
  guaranteed compliance outcome, no board authority.
- The review stage is what makes it a service rather than a project.

**Evidence Status.** Method names and outputs are verbatim from
`config('emplowise.methodology')`.

## Slide 04: Business model

**Slide Goal.** Answer "how do you earn, who pays, and why do they stay" with the
published numbers.

**Approved Ground and Layout.** White `#FFFFFF`, native three-column tier table
across the middle, headline above, one note line below, primary lockup top left.

**On-Slide WH Content.**

Headline: *Published retainers priced by headcount*

- **How we earn.** Monthly retainers by headcount: 40,000, 75,000, or a custom
  enterprise quote.
- **What tiers include.** Fourteen services across payroll, performance and
  policy, delivered remotely.
- **Who pays, who enters free.** SMEs pay monthly; the audit and EmploStart are
  free.
- **Why clients retain.** The payroll calendar and the review cycle live with
  Emplowise.

**Speaker Notes.**

- Starter: up to 20 employees, PKR 40,000 per month. Business: up to 50, PKR
  75,000 per month. Enterprise: 50+, custom and scoped after the audit.
- Package-by-package inclusions are still [To Verify: exact inclusions and limits
  per tier, confirmed in writing after the Free HR Audit].
- Recruitment fee treatment, payroll tax scope and legal qualifications are also
  confirmed in writing after the audit. Say so before a buyer asks.
- Retention comes from cadence: payroll and review dates sit in Emplowise's
  calendar, not the founder's.

**Evidence Status.** Prices and capacities are tier 5 confirmed business facts.
Inclusions are open, tracked as ledger items 1 to 3.

## Slide 05: Market opportunity

**Slide Goal.** Define the wedge qualitatively, without borrowing a market size
the business has not sourced.

**Approved Ground and Layout.** Navy `#081B3E`, reverse lockup, three stepped
wedge cards rising left to right, headline upper left. No chart axes, no bubble
graphic.

**On-Slide WH Content.**

Headline: *A clear wedge between DIY and enterprise*

- **Who.** Pakistani SMEs of five to one hundred staff with no HR owner.
- **Where.** Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad, where hiring outpaces documentation.
- **Why now.** Labour expectations, payroll scrutiny and founder bandwidth are
  all tightening.
- **What wedge.** Take the 10-to-50 band agencies ignore and a single hire cannot
  serve.

**Speaker Notes.**

- TAM, SAM and SOM are not on the slide on purpose. [To Verify: a bottom-up
  estimate for remote HR spend in Pakistan, built from SME counts, target
  headcount bands and a defensible price, with each source dated].

  arithmetic.
- The wedge is a service band, not a technology claim. The AI division is
  pre-launch and is not the entry product.
- Expansion outside Pakistan is planned, not modelled.

**Evidence Status.** Tier 5 positioning plus tier 6 hypothesis. No sourced market
size exists yet; ledger item 10.

## Slide 06: Go-to-market

**Slide Goal.** Explain the entry point, the channels and the conversion path
from free audit to retainer.

**Approved Ground and Layout.** Off-white `#F7F7F7`, three-stage flow with
arrows (audit, gap map, retainer), headline above, primary lockup top left.

**On-Slide WH Content.**

Headline: *Audit first, then EmploStart, then retainers*

- **Who is the beachhead.** Founders introduced through the free HR audit, met by
  one named lead.
- **Where we reach them.** The website, LinkedIn, referral partners and the audit
  form.
- **How it converts.** The audit produces a gap map; the map names the retainer.
- **When phases scale.** Phase one is Pakistan; expansion follows proven
  retention.

**Speaker Notes.**

- The audit is the only verified contact channel in this build. Direct phone and
  inbox details are [To Verify, confirmed in writing after the audit].
- EmploStart is a public good and a demonstration of method, not a lead capture
  device. It asks for no account.
- Channel priorities today: organic search on the nine Phase I pages, LinkedIn,
  and warm referrals. Paid acquisition is untested.
- Conversion is measured as audits completed per month and retainers signed per
  quarter.

**Evidence Status.** Channel list and audit flow are tier 5 operational facts.
Conversion rates are not measured yet, so none are shown.

**On-Slide WH Content.**

**Speaker Notes.**

- Outputs today: 60 tests and 827 assertions, 22 parity fixtures, and a static

- [To Verify: audits completed per month, retainers signed, and any client willing

  traction. [To Verify: permission and framing.]

**Evidence Status.** Repository counts are tier 1 behavioural evidence about the
build, not about the market. Client results are absent by design.
