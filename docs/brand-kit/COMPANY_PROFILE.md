## 02 The market gap

There are two established options for a Pakistani company with 10 to 100 staff,
and both leave a hole in the middle.

**Informal HR, done by the founder or an office manager.** Cheap in cash, costly
in attention. Contracts live in a folder, payroll inputs arrive by WhatsApp,
leave is tracked in memory, and the first real test is a resignation or a payroll
error. Nobody owns the process because owning HR was never anyone's job.

**Enterprise consultancies and full-time HR hires.** Credible, but priced and
shaped for a larger company. A senior HR hire is a fixed salary plus benefits for
a role that is not full time at 30 staff. A consultancy engagement often ends
with a report rather than a running process.

Emplowise sits between them: a monthly retainer, a named team, written scope, and
execution rather than recommendations. The price is published. The method is the
same for every client. The audit is free because the first useful conversation
should start with the buyer's own gaps, not with a sales deck.

## 04 Philosophy and scope boundaries

**Philosophy.** Empower people. Simplify work. Build momentum.

**Mission.** Everyone knows who owns what.

The work starts from the client's yearly goals and works backwards into HR
structure, not from a policy template. If a process cannot be explained on one
page, it is not finished. Structure is what makes growth calm.

**In scope.** Assessment and a written gap map; policies, SOPs and handbooks;
employee records and documentation; payroll inputs and the payroll calendar;
leave and attendance; onboarding; performance support and manager coaching;
employee relations and conflict support; organisational assessments; monthly HR
reporting; ongoing consultation; recruitment execution; specialist consultant
input; a dedicated HR representative.

**Out of scope, stated plainly.** Emplowise does not act as a law firm, does not
guarantee a legal or compliance outcome, does not guarantee hiring results, does
not take board authority and does not replace the client's management. Payroll
tax and disbursement scope, recruitment fee treatment, legal qualifications,
representative allocation and contract terms are confirmed in writing after the
Free HR Audit. Those boundaries are part of the offer, not small print.

## 05 The four-stage methodology

Every engagement follows the same sequence. The output of each stage is a
document the client keeps.

| Stage | What happens | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Assess | Audit the current state: structure, contracts, records, payroll inputs, leave practice, performance habits, unresolved people issues | A written gap map and a prioritised list of fixes |
| 02 Identify | Separate symptoms from causes; pin down unclear ownership, missing policy coverage and steps that rely on memory | A short action plan with owners and a sequence |
| 03 Improve | Install the fix: policies, SOPs, payroll calendar, onboarding flow, manager coaching, reporting | Running processes, not recommendations |
| 04 Review | Check what held: monthly reporting, quarterly assessment, a standing list of open risks | A review pack for leadership every cycle |

## 09 Measurement philosophy

Three different things, measured differently and never merged into one dashboard.

**Inputs and outputs.** What Emplowise does and produces: audits completed, gap
maps delivered, policies written, payroll cycles closed on the published
calendar, onboarding flows installed, review packs sent. These are directly
observable and are the only things quoted as numbers today.

**Audience effects.** What changes for the client's team: fewer people questions
routed to the founder, managers with a script for difficult conversations, every
employee with a record and a contract that matches reality. Observable through
the client's own experience and stated in the client's words, not in invented
percentages.

**Commercial outcomes.** Revenue, retention, cost per employee, time to hire.
These belong to the client and are reported only when the client supplies a
baseline and agrees to its use. Emplowise does not publish savings it cannot
evidence.

Baseline metrics and verified client results are confirmed in writing after the
Free HR Audit. No invented percentages, savings or client names appear anywhere
in this profile.
