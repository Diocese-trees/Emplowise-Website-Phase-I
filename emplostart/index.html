Three ways in

Start with a questionnaire, your LinkedIn profile, or your CV.

The app opens with one question: how do you want to start? Each lane collects the same readiness picture from a different source and saves it to your own device.

  1. 01

    Diagnostic test and questionnaire

    Answer 12 plain questions about your work, evidence, and targets. It takes about 2 to 3 minutes.

    You get one of four readiness bands, plus the prompts that fit that band. There is no pass or fail, only a starting point.

  2. 02

    LinkedIn profile and gap questions

    Paste your LinkedIn URL and the profile text you want reviewed, then answer follow up questions.

    The app works only from what you paste. It does not visit LinkedIn or scrape your profile, and it asks you what is missing instead of guessing.

  3. 03

    CV upload or PDF-paste repair

    Upload a .docx, .txt, or .md file, or paste text out of a PDF that will not read cleanly.

    Extraction happens in your browser. You see a parse preview and confirm it before anything is saved.

Each lane writes to a candidate vault that stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Shared PC mode is session only: closing the tab wipes the vault.

What runs where

Local by default. AI only when you choose it.

The app is built so the useful work happens in your browser. Artificial intelligence is an optional extra, never the default.

Runs locally in your browser

  • All six modules work with no network call.
  • Nothing you type is uploaded, stored on a server, or sold.
  • No account, no payment, and no advertising inside the tools.

Optional AI, off until you consent

  • AI stays off until you turn it on and accept the consent note.
  • Only the text you send in that moment leaves your browser.
  • Every result has a local fallback, so the tool still works without AI.
