                Prompt library

                14 copy-paste prompts for job search, resumes, interviews, LinkedIn, and career clarity. Fill the brackets with your details and run them in any assistant.

                Yours to adapt
                Job search

                Target role shortlist

                Turn a vague job hunt into a ranked list.

                Act as a career strategist. I am a final year student in [degree] looking for roles in [city or remote]. My strengths are [strengths]. List 8 realistic role titles, rank them by fit, and for each one name the single strongest proof point I should build in the next 30 days.

                Job search

                Cold outreach that gets replies

                Message a hiring manager without sounding generic.

                Write a 90 word LinkedIn message to a hiring manager at [company] for the [role] opening. Reference one specific thing about their product. State what I bring in one sentence. End with a low pressure question. No flattery, no buzzwords.

                Job search

                Referral request to an alum

                Ask for a referral without making it awkward.

                Draft a short message to a university alum at [company] asking about the [role]. Include why I am a fit in two concrete lines, attach nothing, and make saying no easy. Keep it under 110 words.

                Resume

                Bullet rewrite with impact

                Turn a duty into an achievement.

                Rewrite this resume bullet using the pattern action verb, what I did, measurable result. Keep it under 22 words and ATS friendly. If a number is missing, give me a placeholder in brackets. Bullet: [paste bullet]

                Resume

                Project to resume bullet

                Make coursework sound like work.

                Convert this academic project into two resume bullets for a [role] application. Emphasise tools, scope, and outcome. Do not invent metrics. Project details: [paste details]

                Resume

                Gap and keyword audit

                Compare your resume to a job post.

                Compare my resume to this job description. List the 12 most important keywords in the posting, mark which appear in my resume, and rewrite my summary to include the top 5 missing ones naturally. Job description: [paste]. Resume: [paste]

                Interview

                STAR answer builder

                Structure a story before you tell it.

                Turn these rough notes into a STAR answer for the question [question]. Situation in one sentence, task in one sentence, action in three steps, result with a number if I have one. Then give me a 20 second version. Notes: [paste]

                Interview

                Mock interview, one question at a time

                Practice under realistic pressure.

                Act as a strict but fair interviewer for a [role] role at a [company type] company. Ask one question at a time. After each answer, score it out of 10 on structure, evidence, and clarity. Give one fix. Start with the question a real interviewer would open with.

                Interview

                Weakness answer without a cliche

                Answer the hardest question honestly.

                Help me answer what is your greatest weakness for a [role] interview. Use a real weakness I can defend: [weakness]. Show the concrete system I use to manage it. Under 90 words, no humble brag phrasing.

                LinkedIn

                Headline that ranks and reads well

                Fix the line recruiters see first.

                Write 5 LinkedIn headlines for a [role] candidate with skills in [skills]. Each must be under 120 characters, include one searchable keyword, and avoid the words passionate, aspiring, and enthusiast.

                LinkedIn

                About section rewrite

                Replace a biography with a pitch.

                Rewrite my LinkedIn About section in first person, 120 words max. Open with what I do and for whom, give two proof points, close with what I am looking for. No adjectives without evidence. Current text: [paste]

                Career clarity

                Two career paths, compared

                Decide between options with a framework.

                Compare a career in [path A] and [path B] for someone with my background: [background]. Use a table with day to day work, entry requirements, salary trajectory in Pakistan, and risk of automation. End with the three questions I should ask myself.

                Career clarity

                90 day plan for a new job

                Start strong without overpromising.

                Build a 90 day plan for a new [role] at a [company size] company. Split into days 1 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90. For each phase give three actions, one relationship to build, and one thing to avoid.

                Career clarity

                Explain a career gap

                Handle the gap question calmly.

                Write a 60 word answer explaining a [length] career gap caused by [reason]. Be direct, take one useful thing from the period, and pivot to why I am ready now. No apology spiral.
