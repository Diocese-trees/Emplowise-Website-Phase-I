02 Market gap

The gap Emplowise was built to fill.

A Pakistani company with 10 to 100 staff has two established options, and both leave a hole in the middle.

Option one

Informal HR, run on memory.

Cheap in cash, costly in attention. Contracts live in a folder, payroll inputs arrive by WhatsApp, leave is tracked in memory, and the first real test is a resignation or a payroll error. Nobody owns the process because owning HR was never anyone's job.

Option two

Enterprise HR, built for a larger company.

A senior HR hire is a fixed salary plus benefits for a role that is not full time at 30 staff. A consultancy engagement often ends with a report rather than a running process.

Emplowise sits between them: a monthly retainer, a named team, written scope, and execution rather than recommendations. The price is published. The method is the same for every client. The audit is free because the first useful conversation should start with the buyer's own gaps, not with a sales deck.

05 Method

Four stages, one sequence, four documents.

Every engagement follows the same sequence, and the output of each stage is a document the client keeps.

  1. Stage 01

    Assess

    Audit the current state. Structure, contracts, records, payroll inputs, leave practice, performance habits, and unresolved people issues.

    A written gap map and a prioritised list of fixes.
  2. Stage 02

    Identify

    Separate symptoms from causes. Pin down unclear ownership, missing policy coverage, and process steps that rely on memory.

    A short action plan with owners and a sequence.
  3. Stage 03

    Improve

    Install the fix. Policies, SOPs, payroll calendar, onboarding flow, manager coaching, and the reporting that follows.

    Running processes, not recommendations.
  4. Stage 04

    Review

    Check what held. Monthly reporting, quarterly assessment, and a standing list of open risks.

    A review pack for leadership every cycle.

09 Measurement

Three kinds of measurement, never merged.

These are different things, measured differently. Only the first is quoted as a number today.

Inputs and outputs

What Emplowise does and produces.

Audits completed, gap maps delivered, policies written, payroll cycles closed on the published calendar, onboarding flows installed, review packs sent. Directly observable and the only things quoted as numbers.

Audience effects

What changes for the client's team.

Fewer people questions routed to the founder, managers with a script for difficult conversations, every employee with a record and a contract that matches reality. Stated in the client's words, not in invented percentages.

Commercial outcomes

What belongs to the client.

Revenue, retention, cost per employee, time to hire. Reported only when the client supplies a baseline and agrees to its use. Emplowise does not publish savings it cannot evidence.

No invented percentages, savings, or client names appear anywhere in this profile. Baseline metrics and verified client results are confirmed in writing after the Free HR Audit.
