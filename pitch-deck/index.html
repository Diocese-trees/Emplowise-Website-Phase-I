    Remote HR structure for growing Pakistani businesses

    • WhatA remote HR team that installs and then runs payroll, performance and policy.
    • WhoStartups and SMEs in Pakistan, five to one hundred employees, with no senior HR hire.
    • WhyPublished prices, written scope, one named team, and a free four-stage audit.
  2. 02 / 08

    Informal HR breaks at thirty staff

    • What failsContracts, payroll inputs and leave live in memory or a spreadsheet.
    • Who feels itThe founder becomes the HR helpdesk and the escalation point.
    • WherePakistani startups and SMEs growing faster than their documentation.
    • Why alternatives failAgencies deliver reports, senior hires cost a salary, and neither runs the process.
  3. 03 / 08

    One method, four stages, written outputs

    • 01 Assess A written gap map and a prioritised list of fixes.
    • 02 Identify A short action plan with owners and a sequence.
    • 03 Improve Running processes, not recommendations.
    • 04 Review A review pack for leadership every cycle.
    • WhatAssess, identify, improve and review the client's people operations.
    • HowAudit the gap, name owners, install the process, then review it monthly.
    • Why it winsEvery stage ends in a document the client keeps and can run.
